# Camera Requirements Analysis

## Objective
Identify the camera requirements for the Face Recognition Attendance Monitoring System.

## Step 1: Study Project Requirements

Reviewed the project objectives and identified camera-related requirements.

![Project Requirements](images/camera-requirements-01.png)

## Step 2: Define Camera Specifications

The following specifications were identified:

- Resolution: 720p minimum
- Recommended Resolution: 1080p
- Frame Rate: 20–30 FPS
- Indoor operation
- USB or Laptop Webcam

![Camera Specifications](images/camera-requirements-02.png)

## Step 3: Analyze Classroom Environment

Studied camera placement and expected student distance from camera.

![Classroom Analysis](images/camera-requirements-03.png)

## Conclusion

The camera should support real-time face detection with sufficient image quality for recognition.
