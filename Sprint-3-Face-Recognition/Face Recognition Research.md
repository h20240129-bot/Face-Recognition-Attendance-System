## Step 1: Research Available Face Recognition Models

Studied different face recognition approaches:

- LBPH
- Eigen Faces
- Fisher Faces
- Dlib Face Recognition
- InsightFace
- ONNX Buffalo Models

![Model Research](images/model-research.png)

## Step 2: Compare Recognition Models

Comparison was performed based on:

- Accuracy
- Recognition Speed
- Ease of Deployment
- Real-time Performance

## Step 3: Select Face Recognition Framework

Selected InsightFace with Buffalo ONNX Model.

Reasons:

- High recognition accuracy
- Fast inference
- Pre-trained model available
- Suitable for real-time attendance systems

Model Used:

- buffalo_l.onnx
